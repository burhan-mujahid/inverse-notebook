# Community code of conduct

Inverse welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and evidence, and give people room to learn and revise their work.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and communication representing the project.

Report concerns privately to **[burhanmujahid552@gmail.com](mailto:burhanmujahid552@gmail.com)**. Include links and relevant context, and share only information needed to understand the incident. The maintainer will handle reports as discreetly as possible, with information shared only as needed to respond.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request a change in behavior, limit participation, or ban participants depending on severity and repetition. Reports and enforcement decisions will be assessed in context. This project cannot promise a fixed response time.
